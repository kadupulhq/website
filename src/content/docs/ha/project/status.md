Kadupul yana matakin farko na pre-alpha. Akwai lambar tushe, amma babu sigar da ake tallafawa don amfani na ainihi ko hanyar ƙaura da aka tabbatar. Kada a yi amfani da shi a tsarin aiki na ainihi.

Wannan shafi taƙaitaccen bayani ne. Karanta cikakken shafin Turanci don ƙarin bayani. [English](/project/status/)

## Halin aikin

Manhajar ta samo asali daga Cacti 1.2.31. Reshen main yana ɗauke da sabbin ayyukan ci gaba; lts/1.2 yana karɓar gyare-gyare da ƙarfafa tsaro. Manufar sigogi tana shirin v1.3.0 da v1.2.32 ga waɗannan rassan. Lambar sigar da aka shirya ba hujjar cewa an fitar da ita ba ce.

Kadupul zai kula da reshensa na Spine. Har yanzu ana buƙatar rubuta wurin ma’ajiyar lambar da sigogin da aka tabbatar. Dacewar ƙarin manhajoji, samfura, rumbunan bayanai da fayilolin RRD manufa ce da ke buƙatar gwaji.

Duba manufofin ma’ajiyar lambar don alkawuran yanzu. Gwaje-gwaje na atomatik da kayan haɗa fakiti kaɗai ba su tabbatar da amincin ƙaura ba.
