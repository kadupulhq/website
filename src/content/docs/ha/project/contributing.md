Ana iya samun lambar tushe kuma ma'ajiyar tana bayyana tsarin ba da gudunmawa. Karanta [CONTRIBUTING.md](https://github.com/kadupulhq/kadupul/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md) kafin ka fara. Tattauna manyan canje-canje a cikin mai bin diddigin matsala da farko.

## Matsayin aiki

Rike kowane buƙatar ja (pull request) ya mai da hankali kan manufa ɗaya. Sake haifar da kuskure kafin gyara shi, tabbatar da canjin aiki, sannan haɗa umarnin tabbatarwa da sakamakon da aka samu. Kiyaye hanyoyin sadarwa na jama'a sai dai idan canjin ya haɗa da ƙaura da aka rubuta a bayyane.

## Ka'idojin aikin injiniya

Bi ƙa'idodin reshen da kake canzawa. Bincika jagorar mai ba da gudunmawa na yanzu da tsarin CI maimakon zaton cewa duka rassan suna amfani da dokoki iri ɗaya.

### Salo

A kan `main`, fayilolin PHP da aka gyara suna bin PHP-FIG PER-CS 2.0. Sake tsara kowane fayil ɗin da abin ya shafa a cikin wani ƙaddamarwa daban na farin fili kawai sannan duba shi da `tests/tools/check_php_style.sh`. Reshen `lts/1.2` yana adana tsarin asali na sama.

### Bincike na tsaye

Gudanar da binciken da reshen da aka nufa ke buƙata. Wannan rukunin yanar gizon ba ya ba da umarnin matakin PHPStan, tsarin shigar da bayanai ko ƙaura mai sarrafa kanta wanda ma'ajiyar ba ta amince da shi ba.

### Gwaje-gwaje

Tabbatar da canjin aikin da facin ya kawo. Haɗa shari'ar koma-baya don gyara kuskure kuma nuna umarnin da aka yi amfani da su. Cin nasarar gwaje-gwajen da ba su da alaƙa ba ya tabbatar da ingancin gyaran.

### Abubuwan dogaro

Yi bayanin dalilin da yasa ake buƙatar abin dogaro, bincika lasisinsa, kuma sabunta madaidaicin bayanin da fayil ɗin kullewa. Bi ƙa'idodin dogaro da tabbatarwa na ma'ajiyar.

### Ƙaddamarwa da buƙatun ja

Yi amfani da Conventional Commits kuma sanya hannu kan kowane ƙaddamarwa tare da `git commit -s` a ƙarƙashin [Developer Certificate of Origin](https://developercertificate.org/). Raba tsari daga canje-canjen aiki don a iya duba kowannensu da kansa.

## Tsarin da aka gada bai cika waɗannan ƙa'idodin ba

Tsarukan suna ɗauke da tsarin tsarawa da aka gada da hanyoyin aiwatarwa. Canji yana ƙarƙashin dokokin reshen da aka nufa, ba ta hanyar sake rubuta aikin gaba ɗaya ba.

### To wace doka ce ke yin nasara?

Bi `CONTRIBUTING.md` akan reshen da aka nufa. A kan `main`, dokar PER-CS ta fayil ɗin da aka gyara tana aiki; a kan `lts/1.2`, kiyaye salon da aka gada.

## Takaddun Shaida

Tabbatar da da'awar game da ainihin aiwatarwa kuma yi amfani da kayan da lasisinsa ya ba da izinin haɗawa. Kada a kwafi rubutun ɓangare na uku ba tare da duba lasisinsa ba. Ba da rahoton binciken tsaro ta hanyar [tsarin bayar da rahoto na sirri](/ha/project/security/).

## Dacewa

Hanyoyin sadarwa na manhajoji (plugins), samfura, dacewar ma'ajiyar bayanai da bayanan RRD manufofin saki ne. Rubuta duk wani canjin tsari kuma samar da shaidar ƙaura da dacewa. Duba [Dacewa da Cacti](/ha/project/compatibility-with-cacti/).
