Ana rarraba Kadupul a ƙarƙashin **GPL-3.0-or-later**, kamar yadda [lasisinta](https://github.com/kadupulhq/kadupul/blob/main/LICENSE) da [bayanin kunshin](https://github.com/kadupulhq/kadupul/blob/main/composer.json) suka bayyana.

## Dalilin da ya sa sigar 3 take akwai

Lasisin manhajar da aka gada yana ba da izinin GPL siga ta 2 ko wata siga ta gaba. Kadupul tana amfani da zaɓin sigar gaba. Abubuwan da aka samo daga waje suna riƙe da sharuɗɗan lasisansu; lasisin manhaja ba ya maye gurbin waɗannan sanarwar.

## Dalilin da ya sa sigar 3 ta fi zama zaɓi mafi kyau

GPL-3.0-or-later ita ce lasisin rarraba da aikin ya ayyana. Bincika lasisin kowane ɓangaren da aka haɗa yayin sake rarrabawa ko canza abubuwan dogaro.

## Jingina Hakki

Riƙe haƙƙin mallaka da sanarwar lasisi masu dacewa, gami da sanarwar da aka gada. Duba fayilolin da ke cikin sigar da kake rarrabawa maimakon dogaro da ƙididdigar bayanai daga wani reshe daban.

## Takaddun Shaida

Takaddun shaida a cikin rukunin `src/content/docs/` na wannan rukunin yanar gizon suna da lasisin [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). Lambar rukunin yanar gizo, saituna, salo da rubutun ginawa suna ƙarƙashin GPL-3.0-or-later.

Yayin raba takaddun da aka gyara, samar da jingina, nuna canje-canje kuma bi sharuɗɗan ShareAlike na lasisi. Creative Commons ta lissafa GPLv3 a matsayin [lasisi mai jituwa ta hanya ɗaya](https://creativecommons.org/compatible-licenses/) don daidaita kayan BY-SA 4.0. Wannan ba ya ba da izinin sake ba da lasisin gyare-gyaren GPLv3 a matsayin BY-SA 4.0.

Tabbatar da lasisi kafin sake amfani da takaddun ɓangare na uku. Lasisin lambar tushe kadai ba ya ba da izinin kwafin rubutun da aka kiyaye daban.
