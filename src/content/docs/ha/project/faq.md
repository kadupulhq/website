Gajerun amsoshi. Kowane yana haɗawa zuwa shafin da ke bayyana shi gaba ɗaya.

## Game da aikin

### Shin zan iya shigar da shi a yau?

Ana iya samun lambar tushe don haɓakawa da gwaji a keɓe. Babu wani ingantaccen sakin da ake tallafawa ko hanyar shigarwa da aka tabbatar don ainihin aiki.

Shafukan shigarwa da jagorori suna buƙatar tabbatarwa game da lambar da kuke amfani da ita. Duba [Matsayin Aiki](/ha/project/status/).

### Shin ya shirya don ainihin aiki?

A'a. Ma'ajiyar tana bayyana Kadupul a matsayin pre-alpha kuma ba a shirye take don ainihin aiki ba. Matsayin saki ba ya nuna ko wani ya tura lambar ci gaba ba.

### Shin wannan madadin Cacti ne?

Kadupul cokali mai yatsu ne mai cin gashin kansa tare da nasa fifikon ci gaba da hanyoyin tallafi. Yana matakin pre-alpha; ƙaura yana buƙatar tabbatarwa don nau'ikan da ƙarin kayan aikin da ake amfani da su. Duba [Fitowa daga Cacti](/ha/guides/coming-from-cacti/).

### Me yasa aka ware wani aikin daban?

Kadupul yana ba da wuri don maye gurbin ƙananan tsarin, haɓaka layin harshe da canza hanyoyin sadarwa na ciki a ƙarƙashin manufofin sakin sa. Duba [Dalilin da yasa wannan reshe yake akwai](/ha/project/why-this-fork/).

### Daga wace sigar Cacti aka ciro shi?

Manhajar ta samo asali ne daga Cacti 1.2.31. Ma'ajiyar tana kula da rassan `main` da `lts/1.2` tare da manufofin saki daban-daban. Duba [Matsayin Aiki](/ha/project/status/).

### Wane ne ke kula da shi?

Aikin Kadupul yana kula da ma'ajiyar. Yi amfani da mai bin diddigin matsala da [tsarin ba da gudunmawa](/ha/project/contributing/) don aikin aikin.

### Shin akwai taswirar aiki, jadawalin saki, ko manufar tallafi?

Ma'ajiyar tana da manufofin saki, manufar bayar da sigogi da tsarin ba da gudunmawa. Har yanzu babu ingantaccen sakin aikin da ake tallafawa. Duba [Matsayin Aiki](/ha/project/status/) don sanin bambanci tsakanin sigogin da aka tsara da abubuwan da aka buga.

### Shin yana da alaƙa da The Cacti Group?

A'a. Kadupul ba shi da alaƙa da The Cacti Group kuma ba ta amince da shi ba. Yana bayyana kansa a matsayin reshe na Cacti saboda wannan sanarwa ce ta gaskiya game da inda lambar ta fito. Cacti aikinsu ne kuma sunansu ne.

### Menene ma'anar sunan?

Kadupul shine sunan Sinhala na wani kaktus mai fure da daddare. Cacti ya ɗauki sunansa daga dangin tsirrai; Kadupul wani nau'i ne a cikinsa. Sunan yana nuna inda aikin ya fito ba tare da da'awar wakiltar gaba ɗayansa ba. Cikakken bayanin yana cikin [Menene Kadupul](/ha/start/what-kadupul-is/).

## Fitowa daga Cacti

### Shin manhajoji na (plugins) za su yi aiki?

Wannan ita ce manufar. API ɗin manhaja yana ɗaya daga cikin manufofin dacewa guda huɗu. Loda da gudanar da manhajar Cacti ba tare da canji ba dole ne a nuna shi don sigogin da ake amfani da su.

Dacewa buri ne, ba cikakkiyar shaidar kowane manhaja ba. Bincika manhajoji da sigogin da kuka dogara da su kafin tsara ƙaura. Duba [Dacewa da Cacti](/ha/project/compatibility-with-cacti/) da [Fitowa daga Cacti](/ha/guides/coming-from-cacti/).

### Shin fayilolin RRD na za su yi aiki?

Kiyaye tarihin RRD buri ne na dacewa. Fayilolin suna ɗauke da tarihin da ba za ku iya sake ƙirƙira ba. Tabbatar da karanta fayilolin da ke akwai akan kwafi kafin kowane ƙaura.

Duk wani ƙaura da ke neman ka jefar da su kuma ka sake ƙirƙirar su yana neman wani abu da ya kamata ka ƙi. Duba [Fitowa daga Cacti](/ha/guides/coming-from-cacti/).

### Shin samfura na da ma'ajiyar bayanai na za su ci gaba?

An tsara samfura don shigo da su ba tare da canji ba. An tsara tsarin ma'ajiyar bayanai don ya kasance mai sauƙin ganewa da ƙaura ba tare da fitarwa zuwa tsarin tsaka-tsaki ba. Waɗannan buri ne da ke buƙatar tabbatarwa, kamar yadda yake tare da manhajoji da fayilolin RRD.

### Inda Kadupul da Cacti suka bambanta, ta yaya zan sani?

Ta hanyar rubuta shi a bayyane. Yanayin gazawar da aikin ke ƙoƙarin kaucewa ba karkacewa ba ne, wanda shine ainihin ma'anar reshe. Karkacewa ce da ba wanda ya rubuta a takarda, wanda ke mai da kowane sabuntawa ya zama bincike mai wahala.

### Shin yana aiki da Spine?

Kadupul za ta kula da reshen Spine tare da mai tattara PHP. Wurin ma'ajiyarta da ingantattun gine-gine ba a rubuta su a nan ba tukuna. Ana gina Spine kuma a shigar da shi daban. Haɗin gwiwar da aka gada ya haɗa da `poller_type` da saitunan hanya, layin umarni na babban mai tattarawa, saitunan da Spine ke karantawa, da teburin da yake rubutawa.

Tabbatar da nau'ikan Kadupul da Spine ɗinku tare kafin sauyawa ta ƙarshe. Ba da rahoton matsaloli a cikin [mai bin diddigin matsala na Kadupul](https://github.com/kadupulhq/kadupul/issues). Duba [Spine, mai tattara C](/ha/reference/spine/) don tsari da jagorar gini.

### Shin yana aiki da wakilin RRDtool?

Kadupul ba ya amfani da sabis na wakili na ƙungiyar Cacti. Yi amfani da ma'ajiyar RRDtool na gida. [Bayanin wakilin RRDtool](/ha/reference/rrdproxy/) yana rubuta ka'idar abokin ciniki da aka gada; ba zaɓin aiki ne da ake tallafawa ba. Dole ne a kafa wanda Kadupul ta mallaka kuma a tabbatar da shi kafin a rubuta yadda ake amfani da wakili.

### Menene yake buƙata don gudanarwa?

Sabar gidan yanar gizo tare da PHP, MySQL ko MariaDB, RRDtool, da wani abu don gudanar da mai tattarawa akan tsari. Sigogi da ƙarin abubuwan da ake buƙata suna kan [Abubuwan da ake buƙata](/ha/reference/requirements/).

## Batun lasisi

### Wace lasisi ce manhajar take ƙarƙashinta?

GPL-3.0-or-later.

### Cacti tana cewa GPL-2. Ta yaya reshe zai zama GPL-3?

Lasisin manhajar da aka gada a sarari yana ba da izinin GPL siga ta 2 ko wata siga ta gaba. Kadupul tana amfani da zaɓin sigar gaba don rarraba ta. Abubuwan dogaro na waje suna riƙe da sharuɗɗan lasisinsu. Duba [Lasisi](/ha/project/license/).

### Wace lasisi ce wannan takarda take ƙarƙashinta?

CC BY-SA 4.0. Lambar rukunin yanar gizon kanta tana kasancewa a ƙarƙashin GPL-3.0-or-later tare da sauran aikin. Sake amfani da rubutun idan kun ba da daraja ga aikin kuma kuka ba da lasisin abin da kuka gina ta hanya ɗaya.

### Daga ina waɗannan shafukan suka fito?

Waɗannan shafukan suna bayyana ainihin aiki daga lambar tushe na manhaja. Rubutun ɓangare na uku yana buƙatar nasa binciken lasisi kafin sake amfani da shi.

Wannan ƙuntatawa ta shafi gudunmawa kuma. Canje-canjen takaddun shaida suna ɗaukar da'awarsu daga ainihin lamba. Duba [Ba da Gudunmawa](/ha/project/contributing/).

## Shiga ciki

### Ta yaya zan ba da rahoton raunin tsaro?

A asirce, ta hanyar GitHub Security Advisories akan ma'ajiyar da abin ya shafa. Ba a matsayin matsalar jama'a ba.

Lambar da ba a fitar ba za a iya tura ta kuma tana iya ƙunsar lahani. Ba da rahoto a asirce ba tare da la'akari da matsayin saki ba. Duba [Manufar tsaro](/ha/project/security/).

### Raunin da na samu a nan yana iya shafar Cacti kuma. Me zai faru?

Ba da rahoton shi a asirce ga Kadupul kuma haɗa abin da kuka sani game da lambar da aka raba. Masu kula da Kadupul suna gudanar da rarrabawa da gyara don Kadupul a ƙarƙashin [manufar tsaro ta ma'ajiya](https://github.com/kadupulhq/kadupul/blob/main/SECURITY.md).

### Ta yaya zan ba da gudunmawa?

Karanta [Ba da Gudunmawa](/ha/project/contributing/) da jagorar mai ba da gudunmawa a kan reshen da kake nufa. Tattauna manyan canje-canje kafin aiwatar da su.

### Na sami wani abu da ba daidai ba a wannan rukunin yanar gizon.

Yana da kyau sosai. An rubuta kowane shafi a nan daga lambar tushe, kuma duk wani abu da ba daidai ba yana da kuskure a kan gaskiyar sa. Kowane shafi yana da hanyar haɗin gyara.
